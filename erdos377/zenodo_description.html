Let Bn = C(2n,n). Theorem 4 of Erdős, Graham, Ruzsa and Straus, On the prime factors of C(2n,n), Math. Comp. 29 (1975), 83–92, states for α < 1 that #{m ≤ nα : m ∤ Bn} = c(α)nα + o(nα) with c(α) → 1 as α → 0, and that c(α) can be explicitly calculated. As printed, the statement has no “almost all n” qualifier (it occurs only in the preceding display), and the constant that tends to 1 is the divisor constant, not the nondivisor one.

For every fixed 0 < α ≤ 1/2 we prove that the proportions of m ≤ nα that divide, and that do not divide, Bn converge in natural density to explicit constants cD(α) and cN(α) = 1 − cD(α), given by an absolutely convergent alternating series of simplex integrals with an effective truncation bound; cN(α) → 0 and cD(α) → 1 as α → 0. Neither proportion has a limit along all n in this range (n = 2h makes every multiple of 4 a nondivisor). Certified rational enclosures are given at α = 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 1/5, e.g. cN(1/2) ∈ [0.160401041573, 0.160401748642]. The localized moment input taken from the 1975 paper is proved here in the uniform form that the argument needs. Nothing is claimed for 1/2 < α < 1, and no effective exceptional-set rate in n is given. This note does not address Erdős Problem #377 itself (boundedness of the sum of 1/p over primes p ≤ n not dividing Bn).

Literature. In a bounded search completed on 10 October 2026 we did not locate an earlier published correction of the printed Theorem 4, or an earlier explicit evaluation of these densities; the MathSciNet review of the 1975 paper was not accessible to us. Related results on densities of indices n with divisibility properties of Bn (Pomerance 2015 and 2026, Sanna 2018, Ford–Konyagin 2021) concern a different quantity.
