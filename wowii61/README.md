v2 is a correction release. Compared with v1 (differences below were read off the
two published PDFs, not off local source):

- **Overstated certification markers withdrawn.** Seven statements — Lemma 4.1,
  Lemma 4.3, Lemma 5.1, Observation 5.2, Fact 5.3, Lemma 5.4 and Theorem 6.1 —
  carried the marker "certified: two review rounds, distinct model families, zero
  mathematics defects" in v1. They did not have that review history. In v2 they
  carry "elementary; proof reproduced in full" and make no review claim, and the
  certification ledger table was rebuilt to match.
- **The τ = 3 result is qualified.** The abstract now says the τ = 3 case is settled
  "by a long case analysis of which this paper gives the skeleton and not every
  branch."
- **The meaning of the certified marker is restated** as a statement about a review
  process, not about correctness.
- One reference cited in v1 but not used was dropped.
