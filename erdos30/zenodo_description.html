An explicit second-order bound for Sidon sets
Haoyu Chen (independent researcher).

Local draft. Coordinator compilation is pending. This description does not assert a publication, DOI, or compiled PDF.

Let F(N) be the maximum cardinality of a Sidon subset of {1,…,N}, with all sums a+b for a≤b distinct, including doubled sums. We prove that, for every integer N≥1204=207,360,000,

F(N) ≤ √N + (2√2/3)N1/4 + 1.

The proof uses a weighted Erdős–Turán energy argument with h(t)=2(1−t) on [0,1], zero elsewhere, and its autocorrelation. An exact half-line construction supplies a total boundary correction of 2/3. The additive constant and onset are explicit, and a monotonicity argument covers all larger N. Erdős's conjecture F(N)=√N+Oε(Nε) for every ε>0 remains open.
