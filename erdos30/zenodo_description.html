An explicit second-order bound for Sidon sets
Haoyu Chen (independent researcher).

Let F(N) be the maximum cardinality of a Sidon subset of {1,…,N}, with all sums a+b for a≤b distinct, including doubled sums. We prove that, for every integer N≥1204=207,360,000,

F(N) ≤ √N + (2√2/3)N1/4 + 1.

The proof is a weighted Erdős–Turán energy argument with h(t)=2(1−t) on [0,1] and its autocorrelation as kernel. The capacity of an interval of length L for this kernel is L+2/3 up to an exponentially small error (instead of the L+1 behind Lindström's bound), at the price of a diagonal cost 4/3. The additive constant and the onset are explicit. Erdős's conjecture F(N)=√N+Oε(Nε) remains open.

Optimality within the method (version 2). For every even nonnegative kernel f, continuous and vanishing at infinity, with integral 1: liminfL→∞ (Cf(L) − L) ≥ 8/(9 f(0)), with equality for the ramp autocorrelation. Hence no fixed kernel in this scalar capacity argument gives a coefficient below √(8/9) = 2√2/3. All statements and proofs of version 1 are unchanged in version 2.

Comparison. A bounded public-source comparison dated 2026-10-02 found no earlier statement of the bound or of the optimality theorem. Located comparison coefficients: 0.9434925907… (Hou–Zhao, preprint) and 0.943006169985179… (an unrefereed repository claim, not replayed), both with an O(1) remainder. The same number 2√2/3 occurs in a different LM-ruler diameter theorem of Gupta–O'Bryant; its objects and conclusion differ. A bounded search does not establish priority.
