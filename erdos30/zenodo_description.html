An explicit second-order bound for Sidon sets
Haoyu Chen (independent researcher).

Let F(N) be the maximum cardinality of a Sidon subset of {1,…,N}, with all sums a+b for a≤b distinct, including doubled sums. We prove that, for every integer N≥4,600,000,

F(N) ≤ √N + (2√2/3)N1/4 + 1.

The proof is a weighted Erdős–Turán energy argument with h(t)=2(1−t) on [0,1] and its autocorrelation as kernel. The capacity of an interval of length L for this kernel is L+2/3 up to an exponentially small error (instead of the L+1 behind Lindström's bound), at the price of a diagonal cost 4/3. The additive constant and the onset are explicit. Erdős's conjecture F(N)=√N+Oε(Nε) remains open.

Version 3 (2026-10-10). The onset is lowered from 1204=207,360,000 (versions 1–2) to 4,600,000 by a new numerical absorption in Section 6; all other statements and proofs are unchanged. Version 3 also adds Section 8.6 on independent work of John Akwei on the same constant (see Comparison).

Optimality within the method (version 2). For every even nonnegative kernel f, continuous and vanishing at infinity, with integral 1: liminfL→∞ (Cf(L) − L) ≥ 8/(9 f(0)), with equality for the ramp autocorrelation. Hence no fixed kernel in this scalar capacity argument gives a coefficient below √(8/9) = 2√2/3. All statements and proofs of version 1 are unchanged in version 2.

Comparison. A bounded public-source comparison dated 2026-10-02 found no earlier statement of the bound or of the optimality theorem. Located comparison coefficients: 0.9434925907… (Hou–Zhao, preprint) and 0.943006169985179… (an unrefereed repository claim, not replayed), both with an O(1) remainder. The same number 2√2/3 occurs in a different LM-ruler diameter theorem of Gupta–O'Bryant; its objects and conclusion differ. A bounded search does not establish priority. Independent work (added in version 3): a GitHub manuscript by John Akwei dated 1 October 2026, one day before version 1 and expanded on 5 October, proves that every symmetric or two-sided Hou–Zhao covering certificate has γ≥2√2/3, the infimum being attained in the continuum by the ramp, and reports an unrefereed certificate γ<0.942809522 with an O(1) remainder and no explicit onset. Our optimality theorem concerns a different object (positive capacity over all even nonnegative kernels); no implication in either direction is known. We learned of this manuscript on 9 October 2026, after versions 1 and 2 had been published.
